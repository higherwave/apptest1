Privacy Policy

Signal Flow · Last updated October 23, 2026

Welcome to Signal Flow. This website is a development, testing, and demonstration environment used solely to showcase technical features and test functionality, including user authentication via Google Sign-In.

Because this is a testing environment, please do not use real personal data, sensitive information, or production credentials when interacting with this site.

Who runs this site

Signal Flow is run by Higherwave (higherwave.org) as a private, invitation-only demonstration of a research-to-requirements tool. It is not a commercial service, and it is shared only with people who have been invited. Questions about this policy can be sent to david.garrett@higherwave.org.

What Signal Flow is, and why it handles information

Signal Flow lets a team plan a research study, upload interview transcripts, review the themes found in them, and turn those themes into requirements and stories. To keep the demonstration limited to invited people, signing in is done with a Google account.

Information we receive from Google when you sign in

When you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares these basic profile details with Signal Flow:

  • your name,
  • your email address (and whether Google has verified it), and
  • your profile picture address, if you have one.

Signal Flow requests only the basic sign-in scopes (openid, email, and profile). It does not request, read, or store anything from your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google service, and it cannot act on your Google account.

How we use that information

  • To check that your email address has been invited, and to give you the access level (owner, editor, or viewer) that was set for it.
  • To show your name and role in the app, and to record who made a change.
  • To keep you signed in for the length of your session.

We do not use this information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or analytics, and we do not sell it or give it to anyone for those purposes.

Information you enter in the app

Anything you type or upload, such as project names, participant details, and transcript text, is stored so the app can show it back to you. This is a testing environment: please use made-up or sample data only.

AI analysis

Signal Flow can use an AI service to analyze transcripts. When this is switched on, the text of the transcripts in the project being analyzed, together with the study's journey stages and test plan, is sent to Anthropic (anthropic.com) through its API so the model can find themes. Before sending, the app replaces speaker names with labels such as P1 and removes email addresses, phone numbers, and participant names it recognizes; file names are not sent. Per Anthropic's API terms, Anthropic keeps this input and the model's output for 30 days by default for safety and security purposes. Signal Flow has not joined any Anthropic program that shares data for model improvement. Analysis is only run by invited editors and owners, never by visitors using the read-only demo. When AI analysis is switched off, or its monthly budget or credit is used up, the app uses a simulated keyword analysis and nothing is sent to an AI service. Because this is a testing environment, please enter only made-up or sample data.

Cookies

Signal Flow sets one first-party cookie, a session cookie (named like better-auth.session_token), that keeps you signed in for up to seven days or until you sign out. It is needed for sign-in to work. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Where information is stored, and who else handles it

The information above is stored in a Postgres database hosted by Neon (in the United States). The site is hosted by Vercel, which may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses and requests, as part of hosting. Google provides the sign-in service, and Anthropic handles transcript text when AI analysis is on (see above). These providers handle information only to run the service for us. We do not share your information with anyone else, except if the law requires it.

How long we keep it, and how to have it removed

Because this is a testing environment, we keep information only while the environment exists, and it may be reset or deleted at any time without notice. An owner can remove your access on the Members page, which also ends your session. To have your name, email address, and sign-in record deleted, email david.garrett@higherwave.org and we will remove them. You can also withdraw Signal Flow's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Signal Flow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
