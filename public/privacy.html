What {{appName}} is, and why it handles information

{{appName}} lets a team plan a research study, upload interview transcripts, review the themes found in them, and turn those themes into requirements and stories. To keep the demonstration limited to invited people, signing in is done with a Google account.

Information we receive from Google when you sign in

When you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares these basic profile details with {{appName}}:

  • your name,
  • your email address (and whether Google has verified it), and
  • your profile picture address, if you have one.

{{appName}} requests only the basic sign-in scopes (openid, email, and profile). It does not request, read, or store anything from your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google service, and it cannot act on your Google account.

How we use that information

  • To check that your email address has been invited, and to give you the access level (owner, editor, or viewer) that was set for it.
  • To show your name and role in the app, and to record who made a change.
  • To keep you signed in for the length of your session.

We do not use this information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or analytics, and we do not sell it or give it to anyone for those purposes.

Information you enter in the app

Anything you type or upload, such as project names, participant details, and transcript text, is stored so the app can show it back to you. This is a testing environment: please use made-up or sample data only.

Prototype thumbnails

When an editor links a prototype in the Design step, our server opens that link once, on our own systems, to take a small picture of the page for the table. The picture is stored with the requirement and the link is not sent to anyone else. A page that needs a sign-in shows the sign-in page, so use the upload option for private prototypes. Private network addresses are never visited. An owner can switch this off.

AI features

{{appName}} can use an AI service, Anthropic (anthropic.com) through its API, for three jobs. Each is switched on or off separately from the rest of the app, and each is started only by an invited editor or owner, never by a visitor using the read-only demo.

  • Analysis. The text of the transcripts in the project being analyzed, each labeled as a session with a customer or a conversation with your own team, together with the study's journey stages, the project description, the questions to ask and tasks to observe from the task list, and the names, short definitions, and alternative names of the experience categories your team keeps, is sent so the model can find themes and say which experience category each is about.
  • Drafting requirements. To write each step of a requirements document, the app sends the study's description and expected outcomes, the roles of the participants, the title, summary, and suggested opportunity of each theme included in the document, one short quote from a participant for each of those themes, and the steps already written in the same document.
  • Matching themes. To avoid listing the same problem twice across projects, the app sends the title, a short description, and the suggested opportunity of each new theme, together with the titles of the themes already shared across projects, and the name of the experience category each belongs to when it has one. The same details are sent when the app compares shared themes to find ones that say the same thing. No transcript text is sent for this.

Before anything is sent, the app removes email addresses and phone numbers and, for transcripts, replaces speaker names with labels such as P1 and removes participant names it recognizes; file names are not sent. Your name, email address, and profile picture from Google are never sent to an AI service. Per Anthropic's API terms, Anthropic keeps this input and the model's output for 30 days by default for safety and security purposes. {{appName}} has not joined any Anthropic program that shares data for model improvement. When AI is switched off, or its monthly budget or credit is used up, the app uses a simulated keyword version of these steps and nothing is sent to an AI service. Because this is a testing environment, please enter only made-up or sample data.

Cookies

{{appName}} sets one first-party cookie, a session cookie (named like better-auth.session_token), that keeps you signed in for up to seven days or until you sign out. It is needed for sign-in to work. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

These providers handle information only to run the service for us. We do not share your information with anyone else, except if the law requires it.
