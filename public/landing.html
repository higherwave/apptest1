Signal Flow Sign in

From user research to requirements.

Signal Flow helps a team plan a research study, upload interview transcripts, see the themes in them with the evidence behind each, and turn those themes into requirements and stories ready to carry into a tracker such as Jira.

Sign in Explore the read-only demo

Sign-in is by invitation, with a Google account. The demo needs no account.

What it does

Plan the study

Say what is being tested, what you expect to learn, and who you will speak with, all on one page.

Find the themes

Upload Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams transcripts. Each theme keeps the quotes it came from.

See what matters

Themes land on a journey map and a priorities map, ready for your team to review and reorder.

Write and track

Turn themes into a reviewed brief and requirements, then follow the resulting stories across a board.

About this site

Anyone can look around the demonstration without an account. Creating and editing is limited to people who have been invited, who sign in with Google. Signal Flow receives only your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. See the privacy policy for the details.

The analysis in this demonstration is simulated, and nothing you enter is sent to an AI service.

Welcome to Signal Flow. This website is a development, testing, and demonstration environment used solely to showcase technical features and test functionality, including user authentication via Google Sign-In.

Because this is a testing environment, please do not use real personal data, sensitive information, or production credentials when interacting with this site.
