About this site

Anyone can look around the demonstration without an account. Creating and editing is limited to people who have been invited, who sign in with Google. {{appName}} receives only your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. See the privacy policy for the details.

When the AI features are switched on, transcript text and the themes and summaries drawn from it are sent to Anthropic’s API, with names and contact details masked first. Otherwise the analysis is simulated and nothing is sent to an AI service. Please use made-up or sample data only. The privacy policy has the details.
