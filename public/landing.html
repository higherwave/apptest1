Signal Flow

A unified framework for ResearchOps, DesignOps, and ProductOps. Drive decisions and insights with customer themes.

Signal Flow helps teams plan a research study, upload interview transcripts, and leverage AI to turn those customer themes into requirements, stories, and insights.

About this site

Anyone can look around the demonstration without an account. Creating and editing is limited to people who have been invited, who sign in with Google. Signal Flow receives only your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. See the privacy policy for the details.

The analysis in this demonstration is simulated, and nothing you enter is sent to an AI service.
