Signal Flow

From user research to requirements. Signal Flow helps a team plan a research study, upload interview transcripts, review the themes found in them, and turn those themes into requirements and stories ready for a tracker such as Jira.

Open the app

What you can do in it

  • Plan a study: what is being tested, the outcomes expected, and the participants.
  • Upload Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams transcripts and review the themes found in them.
  • See themes on a journey map and a priorities map.
  • Write requirements from the themes and track the resulting stories on a board.

Signing in

Anyone can look around the demonstration without an account. Creating and editing is limited to people who have been invited, who sign in with Google. Signal Flow receives only your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. See the privacy policy for the details.
